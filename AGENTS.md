# Design principles

Apply to every design discussion and decision.

1. **Simplicity and generosity of rules.** Prefer simple, generous rules. A generic rule and a generic design is always better than one that needs separate handling of exceptions and outlier cases.
2. **DESIGN.md is the current design only.** Keep `DESIGN.md` as the single source of truth for the latest design, with no historical noise. Record retired or considered-and-rejected designs (e.g. Beans) in `DESIGN-History.md` instead.
